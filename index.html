The three mistakes

What I talk about at the end of every video

These three turn up in almost every account I open — local service shops and online stores alike. None of them is about bidding.

01

You optimise toward the wrong event

The platform chases the cheapest form fill it can find — and the cheapest one on earth is a person who was never going to pay. Send back the booked job, not the click.

Costs you: a lead count that looks great and an empty bay
02

You pay for traffic that isn't searching

Search Partners and Display are on by default. Half the budget leaves search before anyone types your service. Cheap clicks, respectable CTR, silent phone.

Costs you: more than half the budget, every month
03

Nobody measures what happens after the click

Missed calls, a six-hour reply, a page that loads in six seconds. Google did its job and handed you the person. The loss happens on your side of the line.

Costs you: the customers you already paid for
The bays are the easy part.
